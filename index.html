How It Works

Agents are reminded early, often, and automatically.

We cross-reference your office roster against GREC every month and send each agent personalized renewal reminders on a structured timeline — no manual work required.

11 months out

Early heads-up

Agents are notified well in advance, with their current CE hours, Law completion, Code of Ethics, and Fair Housing status included.

6 months out

Progress check-in

A second reminder with updated CE status — time to start completing hours if they haven't already.

3 months out

Urgent reminder

Clear, direct nudge. This is when most agents take action.

2 & 1 month out

Final notices

Last-chance reminders for anyone who hasn't renewed. Leadership is looped in so no one falls through the cracks.

Every month

Leadership report

A full compliance report delivered to your team — who's current, who's behind, and who's been reminded. Read receipts available on request.
