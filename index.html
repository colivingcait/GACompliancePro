The problem

Every broker knows: busy agents need reminding about their CEs.

GREC doesn't send renewal reminders. In some offices, a staff member checks GREC every month and emails agents one by one. In others, nobody does, and agents are left to remember on their own. Either way, it works… until it doesn't.

Manually navigating GREC, building spreadsheets, and sending individual emails is hours of work every single month.

An agent practicing under a lapsed license exposes the broker of record to fines, complaints, and closing complications.

Busy agents don't always remember their renewal deadline. Without reminders, lapses happen — even to good agents.

When the person who tracks compliance is out, nothing gets checked. Agents don't find out until it's too late.

How it works

Agents are reminded early, often, and automatically.

We cross-reference your office roster against GREC every single month and send each agent personalized renewal reminders for their remaining CEs, Law, Broker CEs, COE/FH on a structured timeline — no manual work from your team required.

  1. 1

    Year out

    Early heads-up

    Agents are notified well in advance, so they have time to sign up for their classes and complete them.

  2. 6

    Months out

    Progress check-in

    A second reminder with updated CE status — time to start completing hours if they haven't already.

  3. 3

    Months out

    Urgent reminder

    Clear, direct nudge. This is when most agents take action on their CEs.

  4. 2 & 1

    Months out

    Final notices

    Last-chance reminders for anyone who hasn't renewed. Leadership is looped in so no one falls through the cracks.

What's included

Everything your office needs. Nothing it doesn't.

01 Tracking

Automated renewal tracking

We pull directly from GREC every month and track CE, Law, Code of Ethics and Fair Housing against both GREC and NAR requirements. When GREC, GAR or NAR rules change, we update them.

02 Reminders

Personalized agent and broker emails

Agents and brokers each get their own status, including the new broker CE requirements. Notification timelines and leadership loop-ins adjust to how your office runs.

03 Reporting

Monthly leadership report

Delivered to your team every month, on time: who was reminded, their renewal window and CE status, and anyone we couldn't reach because of a GREC mismatch, a missing roster entry or a bounced email. Read receipts available on request.

04 Lapse alerts

Know about lapses within days

Most licenses lapse silently. Our report flags any agent who drops off GREC near their deadline, so you find out within a few days, not in the middle of their next closing.
